How RideFlow Works

  1. A passenger requests a ride with a pickup point and a destination.
  2. A dispatcher assigns an available driver to the request.
  3. The driver accepts, arrives, picks up the passenger, and completes the trip.
  4. The passenger rates the driver and can report any concerns.

Main Features

  • Instant ride requests with a transparent fare estimate
  • Live ride status from request to completion
  • Driver ratings and passenger feedback
  • Complaint handling and emergency assistance

Safety

  • Driver license and vehicle details are checked before a driver can take rides.
  • Passengers can send an emergency request during an active trip.
  • Ride details, ratings, and complaints are kept on record for review.
